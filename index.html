openclaw-email

openclaw-email is a personal tool that helps its owner manage one dedicated Gmail inbox from a home assistant. It sorts incoming mail into categories, flags messages that need a reply, filters spam, and saves suggested replies as Gmail drafts for the owner to review.

It runs only on the owner's own computer and is used only by its owner. It never sends email: every reply is saved as a draft and sent, or deleted, by hand.
